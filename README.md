# Compete for Mars
Compete for Mars is the game created for the Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer challenge at the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge in Zaragoza. The authors are Lucía Hernando, Diego Gómez, Gabriel Sanjuan, Víctor de Francisco, and Didac Medal, from team C-Moon Dynamics.

Compete for Mars is a online multiplayer game (up to 4 players) set in a Martian base, built as a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer. Kids compete in a series of mini-games, such as driving a rover across real Martian terrain, repairing an electrical circuit, suiting up for an EVA or surviving a solar storm, to find out who survives and makes it back to Earth. Along the way, players must manage limited resources and deal with sabotage and deception from rivals. Each mini-game is built around real NASA science and mission data, so children learn how life-support systems, space weather, radiation and spacesuits actually work while they play. Our goal is to make space operations approachable and exciting for young audiences and to inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers.
